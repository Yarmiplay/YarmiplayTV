# Help test YarmiplayTV

Google needs 12 people to test YarmiplayTV for 14 days before it can go on Google Play. Have an Android phone,
tablet or Google TV? Joining takes two minutes.

## Join

1. **Join the testers group**
   Use the Google account that's on your phone or TV.
   [Join group](https://groups.google.com/g/yarmiplaytv-testers)
2. **Become a tester**
   Sign in with the same account.
   [Become a tester](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.yarmiplaytv)
3. **Install YarmiplayTV**
   On your phone, tablet or Google TV.
   [Open Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.yarmiplaytv)

Does step 2 say the app isn't available? The test hasn't opened yet; try again in a few days.

## Then

- Keep the app for 14 days. Google only counts testers who stay that long.
- Open it now and then: watch something with friends, or join the same room from two devices (server
  `syncplay.pl`, port `8999`, any room name).
- Installed YarmiplayTV from our download page before? Uninstall that version first.

## Feedback or leaving

- Tell us what breaks or confuses you in the tester feedback on the app's Play Store page, or on
  [GitHub](https://github.com/Yarmiplay/YarmiplayTV/issues).
- To stop, choose **Leave the program** on the [testing page](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.yarmiplaytv).

The app collects no data: see the [privacy policy](../privacy/).
